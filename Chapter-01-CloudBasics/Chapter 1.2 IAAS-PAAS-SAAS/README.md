
## What is IAAS?

IAAS is Infrastucture as a service  and it is the provision of raw infrastucture such as storage, servers, netowrking, etc .


## Who is IAAS For?
Entities and companies that more customization and config over their os and servers

Example
- PDO
- Banks
- Government Entities

## Who Provides IAAS?
-AWS
-AZURE
-Oman DAta Park
-Data2cloud

##What is PAAS?
It is the provisions of a platform to assist in creation and  maintainence of  software throughout its software Development LifeCycle

## Who is IAAS For?
PaaS is used by  software teams to build, test, and run custom applications and databases without managing physical hardware.
Example
- Rihal
- Bahwan CyberTek
- Thawani
- OMPay
  
## Who Provides PAAS?
- OpenShift
- Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)
